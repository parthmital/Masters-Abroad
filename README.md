# Masters Abroad 2027: Computer Science Decision Guide

**Data status:** Researched and cross-checked on **14 September 2026** (India time). Figures come from official university fee schedules, programme pages, and government immigration and labour-market sources. Anything not officially published for the 2027 cycle is labelled; nothing unlabelled is guessed.

**Currency:** All money is shown in **Indian rupees**, converted at Xe mid-market rates on 14 September 2026 (section 5.1). The original currency is shown next to key figures so each one can be checked at source.

**Profile baseline:** Vellore Institute of Technology Bachelor of Technology Computer Science and Engineering (2023 to 2027), cumulative grade point average 8.75/10, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure 2025 Generative Artificial Intelligence Professional (July 2026). Experience:

- Production full-stack enterprise resource planning migration (Next.js, PostgreSQL, 587 tests)
- Artificial Intelligence Engineer internship: on-device computer vision, TensorFlow Lite quantisation, You Only Look Once version 8, optical character recognition/large language model pipelines on Amazon Web Services Bedrock
- NeuroScan AI: a 21-architecture medical-imaging benchmark, with 2 manuscripts under review

International English Language Testing System 8.0 is assumed and no Graduate Record Examinations score is assumed. The degree finishes mid-2027, so the target is a **September 2027** intake (Canada, Ireland) or **February 2028** intake (Australia).

**Ranking criteria, in order of weight** (full method in section 3):

1. **Permanent settlement prospects** (20%): post-study work length and a realistic permanent residence route for software/Artificial Intelligence graduates.
2. **Finances** (20%): total net cost in Indian rupees.
3. **Job prospects** (17.5%): depth of the local tech labour market plus built-in employer contact (co-op, internship, practicum).
4. **Return on investment** (15%): net cost against official pay evidence for the destination, adjusted for time out of work.
5. **University value and prestige** (12.5%): Quacquarelli Symonds 2027 standing and institution type.
6. **Coursework breadth** (7.5%): general Computer Science core with Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning/Data Science electives over narrow specialisations.
7. **Project-oriented track** (7.5%): co-op, internship, practicum or team project over research dissertations.

**Filters (unchanged, applied before ranking):** thesis-only and conversion programmes are excluded; specialisations are limited to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning or Data Science to match the resume; Australian programmes must meet the 2-year 485 study requirement (section 10).

**Scope assumption:** The destination set stays at **Canada, Ireland and Australia**, because these are the settlement-focused targets of this plan. Every university, programme, cost, deadline and ranking inside those countries was re-researched from scratch.

## 1. Decision Summary

- **Country order:**
  1. **Ireland** (8.17/10)
  2. **Canada** (7.83/10)
  3. **Australia** (5.83/10)
- **Why Ireland leads:**
  - Strongest settlement score (8.0): 24 months of Stamp 1G ([84]), Standard Occupational Classification 2136 on the Critical Skills Occupations List ([87]), and Stamp 4 after 21 months on a Critical Skills Employment Permit ([88]).
  - 1-year taught degrees keep the top four Irish programmes at 50.55 to 62.92 lakh Indian rupees net, giving the best return on investment in this guide against Central Statistics Office information and communications technology sector earnings ([90]).
  - Four of the top five programmes (University College Dublin, Dublin City University, Trinity College Dublin, Technological University Dublin) share the Dublin job market.
- **Why Canada is second:**
  - The 3-year open Post-Graduation Work Permit is the strongest post-study permit ([15]), and the co-op and internship programmes give the best built-in job access.
  - Top-brand options (University of Toronto, University of British Columbia, Waterloo) score highly on prestige and jobs.
  - The permanent residence outlook for software graduates tightened sharply in 2025 and 2026, which caps the settlement score at 6.5:
    - The 2026 science, technology, engineering and mathematics draw category does not list software or data science occupations ([18]).
    - Ontario removed its Masters Graduate stream on 26 June 2026 ([22]).
    - British Columbia says it will not launch new student streams ([21]).
    - Canadian Experience Class cutoffs in 2026 were Comprehensive Ranking System 507 to 523 ([19]).
- **Why Australia is third:**
  - The 3-year Subclass 485 for Indian master's graduates is strong ([146]).
  - But a 485-compliant 2-year degree costs 1.10 to 1.42 crore Indian rupees net, so finance scores fall to 0.7 to 3.7 and return on investment to 1.1 to 3.1.
  - Graduate visa and student visa charges rose on 1 July 2026 ([140], [145]).
- **Top 5 programmes overall:**
  1. **University College Dublin Master of Science in Computer Science (Negotiated Learning)**, 62.92 lakh Indian rupees, score 8.54
  2. **University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing**, 69.51 lakh Indian rupees net of internship pay, score 8.20 (5 to 10% offer rate)
  3. **Dublin City University Master of Science in Computing (Artificial Intelligence or Data Analytics major)**, 50.55 lakh Indian rupees net, score 8.04
  4. **Trinity College Dublin Master of Science in Computer Science (Intelligent Systems or Data Science)**, 58.51 lakh Indian rupees, score 7.94
  5. **Technological University Dublin Master of Science in Computing (Advanced Software Development or Data Science)**, 51.82 lakh Indian rupees, score 7.80
- **Best value (finances and return on investment) with a credible settlement route:**
  - **Dublin City University DC836:** 50.55 lakh Indian rupees, mandatory practicum worth 30 European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System credits ([103]).
  - **Technological University Dublin TU059:** 51.82 lakh Indian rupees, team project option ([119]).
  - **University of Ottawa Master of Computer Science:** 45.81 lakh Indian rupees (fee to be verified), mandatory project, optional co-op ([48]).
- **Best general-Computer Science plus project fit (lower-weight criteria):**
  - **University College Dublin T150:** about 80 taught modules across 5 streams, no dissertation, competitive internship ([100]).
  - **University of Ottawa Master of Computer Science:** coursework plus a mandatory 6-unit project, optional co-op ([48]).
  - **Concordia Master of Applied Computer Science:** 45-credit course-based degree, optional co-op ([57]).
- **Best Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning/Data Science project fit:**
  - **Simon Fraser University Master of Science in Big Data:** mandatory co-op, two big-data labs ([40], [42]).
  - **Dublin City University DC836 Artificial Intelligence major:** practicum worth 30 European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System credits ([103]).
  - **Waterloo Master of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence:** one co-op term ([60]).

| **[Official snippet]**     | Official domain, but the page blocked full reading; the fact comes from the page's indexed text.                                                                 |

Rules applied:

- **Fees:** 2027/28 fees are unpublished for almost every programme on 14 September 2026, so the latest official fee is used and labelled with its year.
- **Admission statistics:** acceptance rates are not estimated. Only officially published admission statistics are quoted.
- **Salaries:** official government wage data only (section 8). There are no programme-level "target salaries".
- **On-campus job pay:** hourly rates and "cost offset" percentages are omitted unless officially published. Only legally defined work-hour limits are included.
- **Rankings:** Quacquarelli Symonds World University Rankings 2027 (released June 2026). Where topuniversities.com blocked access, the rank comes from a university press release or a named news source.

## 3. Ranking Method

Each programme gets a weighted score out of 10. The research, costs and filters are unchanged from the previous version; only the criteria, weights and ordering were revised.

### 3.1 Criteria and Weights

The core decision factors (settlement, finances, jobs, return on investment, prestige) carry 85%. Coursework breadth and project track remain criteria but carry 15% together, because they matter less to the outcome than whether the degree is affordable and leads to work and residence.

| Order | Criterion                      | Weight | 10 means                                                                                                           | Low score means                                                |
| ----: | :----------------------------- | -----: | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
|     1 | Permanent settlement prospects |    20% | Long open post-study permit plus a clear, non-lottery permanent residence route for software roles                 | Short permit, permanent residence route closed or points-heavy |
|     2 | Finances                       |    20% | Net cost at or below 40 lakh Indian rupees (linear to 0 at 1.50 crore Indian rupees)                               | High tuition, long duration, expensive city                    |
|     3 | Job prospects                  |  17.5% | Large tech labour market plus mandatory paid co-op or internship with official data                                | Small regional market, no employer contact in degree           |
|     4 | Return on investment           |    15% | Net cost recovered quickly against official pay evidence, short time out of work                                   | Net cost equals several years of benchmark pay                 |
|     5 | University value and prestige  |  12.5% | Quacquarelli Symonds top 50 research university                                                                    | Unranked regional institution                                  |
|     6 | Coursework breadth             |   7.5% | General Computer Science degree with wide core and Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning/Data Science electives | Narrow specialisation, few Computer Science electives          |
|     7 | Project-oriented track         |   7.5% | Mandatory paid co-op or internship, or mandatory industry practicum                                                | Compulsory research dissertation or thesis                     |

**Admission feasibility is no longer scored.** It measures the chance of getting in, not the value of the outcome, so it is handled in the application portfolio (section 13). Published offer rates and entry bars stay in each programme guide.

### 3.2 How Each Criterion Is Scored

**Settlement:** country or area score from the section 7 evidence.

| Country or area                                                                                                         | Score |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----: |
| Ireland                                                                                                                 |   8.0 |
| Canada, outside Quebec                                                                                                  |   6.5 |
| Canada, Quebec (Concordia): Quebec Experience Program graduate route does not cover a 2027 intake; French needed ([25]) |   5.5 |
| Australia, Category 2 regional cities (Adelaide, Canberra)                                                              |   6.5 |
| Australia, major cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane)                                                                   |   5.5 |

**Finances:** 10 × (150 − net cost in lakh Indian rupees) ÷ 110, capped between 0 and 10.

**Job prospects:** city tech-market base plus a work-integration bonus, capped at 10. City bases are the author's judgement of market depth, informed by sections 7 and 8.

| --------: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|         8 | Dublin, Toronto, Vancouver, Sydney, Melbourne                               |
|         7 | Cork, Maynooth (Dublin commuter belt), Ottawa, Montréal, Waterloo, Brisbane |
|         6 | Limerick, Galway, Calgary, Canberra, Adelaide                               |
|         5 | London, Ontario, Halifax                                                    |

| Bonus | Applies to                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|    +2 | Mandatory paid industry internship or co-op with official placement or salary data (University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing, Simon Fraser University)                                                                                                      |
|  +1.5 | Mandatory co-op or experiential term without outcome data (Waterloo Master of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Western Master of Data Analytics)                                                                                                                       |
|    +1 | Optional co-op, competitive internship, mandatory project/internship, or work-integrated learning with industry (University College Dublin, University of Ottawa, Concordia, Dalhousie, Carleton, Windsor, Queensland University of Technology, Australian National University) |
|  +0.5 | Mandatory practicum, internship-or-capstone choice, work-integrated capstone or industry research project (Dublin City University, University of Calgary, University of Sydney, Adelaide)                                                                                       |
|     0 | Team project, dissertation or unverified structure only                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

**Return on investment:** payback ratio = net cost ÷ annual pay benchmark. Score = 10 − 3.5 × (ratio − 0.4) − 0.1 × (programme months beyond 12), capped between 0 and 10.

| Country   | Benchmark (lakh Indian rupees a year) | Basis                                                                                                                                                         |
| :-------- | ------------------------------------: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Ireland   |                                 99.61 | Central Statistics Office average weekly earnings, Information & Communication sector, × 52 ([90])                                                            |
| Canada    |                                 64.57 | Job Bank median, National Occupational Classification 21232 Software developers and programmers ([28])                                                        |
| Australia |                                 54.38 | Core Skills Income Threshold 79,499 Australian dollars [Secondary] ([152]); no Jobs and Skills Australia median was readable, so this is a conservative floor |

The benchmarks are all-experience figures, not graduate starting salaries, and measure different things (a sector average, an occupation median and a visa threshold). Return on investment is a relative indicator only.

**University value and prestige:** Quacquarelli Symonds 2027 scale: top 50 = 10, 51 to 100 = 9, 101 to 150 = 8, 151 to 250 = 6, 251 to 350 = 5, 351 to 450 = 4, 451 to 550 = 3. Rank not found = 3; unranked = 1. +1 (cap 10) where an official Quacquarelli Symonds Subject 2026 Computer Science rank is in the top 50 and the overall score is below 10 (Waterloo). Every institution in this guide is a public university or public technological university; none is a private for-profit college. Technological University Dublin and Atlantic Technological University are technological universities and score on the same Quacquarelli Symonds scale.

**Breadth and project:** the author's rubric judgements, unchanged from the previous version.

### 3.3 Country Score

The mean of each country's top three programme scores.

### 3.4 Data-Confidence Penalty

−0.30 where tuition is not from an official fee schedule. This applies to:

- University of Ottawa, Dalhousie and Windsor (secondary figures)
- University of Calgary (conflicting official extractions)
- Carleton (billing basis estimated)
- Atlantic Technological University (India rate unconfirmed)

### 3.5 Known Limitations

- Job bases, breadth and project scores are judgements. Each programme's criterion scores are shown in sections 6 and 9 so they can be challenged.
- A work-integrated track raises both the job score and the project score, so project-heavy programmes gain slightly more than the 7.5% project weight alone suggests.
- Dropping admission feasibility lifts highly selective programmes (University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing's 5 to 10% offer rate, Galway's First Class entry). Read their ranks as "if admitted".
- University College Cork and University of Ottawa both display 7.60; University College Cork ranks higher on the unrounded score.

### 5.4 How Net Cost Is Calculated

**Net cost = total tuition and published mandatory fees + monthly living × programme months + setup − offsets**

- Only offsets that are **automatic and officially quantified** are deducted:
  - Dublin City University's 4,400 euros fee reduction for 2027/28 ([104])
  - University of Toronto Master of Science in Applied Computing's published average internship salary of 25,000 Canadian dollars for 8 months ([66])
- Competitive scholarships and co-op pay without official salary data are listed but **not deducted**.
- Co-op terms at Simon Fraser University, Waterloo, Concordia, Carleton, University of Ottawa and Dalhousie typically pay wages, but no official salary figure was found for those programmes.
