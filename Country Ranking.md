Costs are tuition only. They exclude rent, food, health cover, visa charges, flights, deposits and an emergency buffer. The range uses the prioritised programmes in `Universities.md` and excludes rows explicitly marked as admission backups.

## Decision notes

- Australia remains first, but the budget is the main constraint. UQ's cognate one-year or 1.5-year path is much cheaper than UNSW, Melbourne or Sydney.
- Ireland remains the strongest cost-to-opportunity option. The one-year format lowers tuition and time away from work, but Dublin housing and a small market are material risks.
- Canada should focus on public universities and verified PGWP-eligible master's programmes. Waterloo's MMath Computer Science is research-based, not a direct coursework option.
- New Zealand fees were previously understated. The corrected serious-programme range is roughly ₹32 lakh to ₹49 lakh, while the graduate technology market is the smallest among the main English-speaking choices.
- The UK Graduate route reduction is now material for a 2027 graduate-visa application. Avoid paying London-level costs for a weak institution.
- The US remains a career-first choice, not a predictable settlement choice. Arizona State's MS Computer Science is not a no-GRE safety for most external applicants.
- Singapore has been downgraded because the 2027 Employment Pass threshold overlaps poorly with official fresh-graduate software vacancy pay. The old list also contained programmes that could not be verified in the stated form.
